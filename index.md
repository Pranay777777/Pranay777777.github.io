# JobOS

JobOS is a personal job-search automation tool. It is built and used by one person, its owner, to organise their own job search. It is not a public service, and no one else can sign up or use it.

What it does for its owner:

- Finds recently posted job listings and filters them against the owner's own criteria.
- Keeps a tracker of jobs and applications in the owner's own Google Sheet.
- Stores tailored resumes and cover letters in the owner's own Google Drive and Docs.
- Drafts and sends job-search emails from the owner's own Gmail, only after the owner has approved them.
